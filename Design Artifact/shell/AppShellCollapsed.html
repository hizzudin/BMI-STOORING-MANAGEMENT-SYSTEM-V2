Collapsed

A floating white capsule on the left holds the BMI logo in a circle, one circular icon per menu item, the expand button and the user circle. Names show as tooltips; waiting counts sit on the icon.

Expanded

Expand widens the same capsule to show names and counts. The choice is saved per user. The main screen keeps its full width and moves aside.

Items come from the 2.4 menu table filtered by the effective capabilities in GET /me, never from the role name (NAV-9). Switch "Viewed as" above to see the rail change.

Main screen first
