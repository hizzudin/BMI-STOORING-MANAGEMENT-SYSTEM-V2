# 4.1 SPK: Surat Perintah Kerja lifecycle (P0, risk: high)

> Part of the BMI Stooring System spec (§4 functional requirements, area SPK). Core rules, roles, state machines, business rules and decisions: [PRD.md](../../../PRD.md). Section numbers and IDs are unchanged from the single-file PRD v2.4.


Goal: every planned or unexpected job becomes an SPK that an Approver authorizes before any work starts. States and transitions: `SM-SPK` (3.2).

| REQ-SPK-010 | P0 | THE SYSTEM SHALL list SPKs in the tabs Active, My steps, Draft, Pending approval, Rejected, Completed, Cancelled and All (with counts), with search, type and vehicle filters and the columns SPK, vehicle, type, status, current step, waiting on, action. | [`SPKList`](<../../../Design Artifact/spk/SPKList.html#SPKList>) | `spks` |  |

The user MAY change the steps. | [`CreateSPKFromAlert`](<../../../Design Artifact/spk/CreateSPKFromAlert.html#CreateSPKFromAlert>), [`VehicleOverview`](<../../../Design Artifact/fleet/VehicleOverview.html#VehicleOverview>) | `maintenance_alerts` |  |
| REQ-SPK-013 | P0 | IF the vehicle is Inactive, THEN THE SYSTEM SHALL refuse to create an SPK for it. | [`CreateSPK`](<../../../Design Artifact/spk/CreateSPK.html#CreateSPK>) | `vehicles.status` |  |
| REQ-SPK-015 | P0 | THE SYSTEM SHALL offer the priorities Low, Normal (default), High and Urgent (`spks.priority`), which are the SPK's urgency label; Urgent and High SPKs SHALL sort first in every list and inbox, and WHEN an Urgent SPK is submitted, THE SYSTEM SHALL notify every Approver at once by in-app and push; priority sets no deadline; late SPKs are alerted by REQ-SCH-007. The same urgency label orders the Mechanic mobile task list (REQ-MOB-010) and SHALL be shown wherever an SPK appears as a colour-coded urgency pill that always carries its text label (status never by colour alone, NFR-A11Y): Urgent as a danger (red) pill, High as a warning (amber) pill, Normal as an info / neutral pill, Low as a neutral pill (DESIGN.md urgency pills, DEC-92). | [CreateSPK](<../../../Design Artifact/spk/CreateSPK.html#CreateSPK>), [SPKList](<../../../Design Artifact/spk/SPKList.html#SPKList>), [Approvals](<../../../Design Artifact/approvals/Approvals.html#Approvals>) | spks.priority | - |
| REQ-SPK-017 | P0 | THE SYSTEM SHALL let a Planner or the System Admin build the steps of an SPK directly in Create SPK, on one screen: the common fields (title, vehicle, category, priority, Vehicle goes to the workshop, description) and a step list. The user adds a step by dragging a module from the palette onto a drop zone between steps, or with Add step; reorders steps by dragging or with Move up and Move down; removes a step; and sets for each step its executor role, verifier, optional flag and details, rendered from the module's creation section (part lines with quantity for Stock, parts to buy for Part Purchase, work to do and assignee for Mechanic Action, positions for Part Replacement, the document for Document). The details move with their step. The user MAY start from an SPK preset, which fills the steps and the common fields, and MAY save the current steps as a preset (REQ-REG-003). A Checks panel shows the rules of REQ-REG-004 live. The form SHALL NOT hard-code any module (REQ-REG-006, DEC-85). | [CreateSPK](<../../../Design Artifact/spk/CreateSPK.html#CreateSPK>), [CreateSPKFromReport](<../../../Design Artifact/spk/CreateSPKFromReport.html#CreateSPKFromReport>), [MobileReportBreakdown](<../../../Design Artifact/mobile/MobileReportBreakdown.html#MobileReportBreakdown>) | assignment\_modules.creation\_schema | REQ-REG-006 |
