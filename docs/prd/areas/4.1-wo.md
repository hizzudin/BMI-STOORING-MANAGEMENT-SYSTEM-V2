# 4.1 WO: Work Order lifecycle (P0, risk: high)

> Part of the BMI Stooring System spec (§4 functional requirements, area WO). Core rules, roles, state machines, business rules and decisions: [PRD.md](../../../PRD.md). Section numbers and IDs are unchanged from the single-file PRD v2.4.


Goal: every planned or unexpected job becomes a WO that an Approver authorizes before any work starts. States and transitions: `SM-WO` (3.2).
