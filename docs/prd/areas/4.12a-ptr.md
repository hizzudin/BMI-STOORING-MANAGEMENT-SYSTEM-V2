# 4.12a PTR: part traceability and performance (P0 data in Release 1, P1 analysis in Release 2, risk: medium)

> Part of the BMI Stooring System spec (§4 functional requirements, area PTR). Core rules, roles, state machines, business rules and decisions: [PRD.md](../../../PRD.md). Section numbers and IDs are the same across the whole spec.

Goal: know, for every tracked part item and every tyre, which vehicle it went to, when it was repaired and when it was replaced or scrapped, with its brand, its vendor and what it cost, so the Planner and management can compare brands, vendors and retread vendors by cost per km and cost per day of use (DEC-112). Tracked parts are the part items of 4.12 (parts with a part item type) and the tyres of 4.14. The data is recorded from go-live in Release 1; the analysis comes in Release 2.
