# 4.13 INV: parts inventory (P0, risk: high)

> Part of the BMI Stooring System spec (§4 functional requirements, area INV). Core rules, roles, state machines, business rules and decisions: [PRD.md](../../../PRD.md). Section numbers and IDs are unchanged from the single-file PRD v2.4.


Goal: every change to part stock has a document, and stock stays accurate. Tyres are never handled here (4.14). States: `SM-GR`, `SM-SA` (3.7).
