# 4.14 TYR: tyres and retread (P0)

> Part of the BMI Stooring System spec (§4 functional requirements, area TYR). Core rules, roles, state machines, business rules and decisions: [PRD.md](../../../PRD.md). Section numbers and IDs are unchanged from the single-file PRD v2.4.


Goal: track every tyre as an individual asset by condition, outside the part catalog, with retread handled outside SPKs. States: `SM-TYRE` (3.6), `SM-TR`, `SM-RD`, `SM-TC` (3.7).

| ID | P | Requirement | Screens | Data | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| REQ-TYR-002 | P0 | THE SYSTEM SHALL give every tyre a generated system code (TY-2611030101) that cannot be typed, edited or reused, and store the serial number printed on the tyre, unique per brand \[A\]; both SHALL show wherever a tyre is listed or chosen. | [`Tyres`](<../../../Design Artifact/inventory/Tyres.html#Tyres>), [`Stock`](<../../../Design Artifact/steps/Stock.html#Stock>), [`Placement`](<../../../Design Artifact/steps/Placement.html#Placement>), [`PartsBaseline`](<../../../Design Artifact/fleet/PartsBaseline.html#PartsBaseline>) | `tyres.tyre_code`, `UNIQUE (brand, serial_number)` |  |
