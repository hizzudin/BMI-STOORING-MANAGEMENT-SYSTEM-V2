# 4.16 DSH: role dashboards (P0)

> Part of the BMI Stooring System spec (§4 functional requirements, area DSH). Core rules, roles, state machines, business rules and decisions: [PRD.md](../../../PRD.md). Section numbers and IDs are unchanged from the single-file PRD v2.4.


Every role lands on a dashboard that shows only work it can act on, and every item opens the exact WO, step or document it names.

| ID | P | Requirement | Screens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| REQ-DSH-002 | P0 | Planner: steps to verify, drafts from alerts, own WOs waiting for approval, WOs not on the schedule, Needs my action, alerts without a WO (each with Create WO). | [`DashboardPlanner`](<../../../Design Artifact/dashboard/Dashboard.html#DashboardPlanner>) |
| REQ-DSH-003 | P0 | Mechanic: steps open, returned and coming (locked), submitted this week, vehicles in the workshop today, Create WO (still needs approval). | [`DashboardMechanic`](<../../../Design Artifact/dashboard/Dashboard.html#DashboardMechanic>) |
| REQ-DSH-004 | P0 | Warehouse Manager: Stock steps open, parts at or below reorder point, tyres needing retread, open dispatches, returned counts, low stock list. | [`DashboardWarehouse`](<../../../Design Artifact/dashboard/Dashboard.html#DashboardWarehouse>) |
