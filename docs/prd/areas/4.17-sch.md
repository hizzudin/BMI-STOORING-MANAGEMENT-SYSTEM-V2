# 4.17 SCH: maintenance schedule (P0)

> Part of the BMI Stooring System spec (§4 functional requirements, area SCH). Core rules, roles, state machines, business rules and decisions: [PRD.md](../../../PRD.md). Section numbers and IDs are unchanged from the single-file PRD v2.4.


Goal: the Planner places WO cards on planned days and the Field Coordinator plans trips around them. The schedule is a plan, never a rule on the WO (`BR-13`). States: `SM-SCH` (3.7).

| ID | P | Requirement | Screens | Data | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| REQ-SCH-001 | P0 | THE SYSTEM SHALL show every open WO (Draft, Pending\_Approval, Active, In\_Progress, Pending\_Verification, Pending\_Amendment) as a card; a card with no day sits in "Not on the schedule". | [`MaintenanceSchedule`](<../../../Design Artifact/schedule/Schedule.html#MaintenanceSchedule>) | `wo_schedule` |  |
| REQ-SCH-002 | P0 | THE SYSTEM SHALL let only the Planner and System Admin drag a card onto a day (week or month view) or to another day at any time; one day can hold several cards. | [`MaintenanceSchedule`](<../../../Design Artifact/schedule/Schedule.html#MaintenanceSchedule>) | `wo_schedule`, `wo_schedule_log` | RL-6 |
| REQ-SCH-003 | P0 | WHEN a planned day ends and the WO is not Completed, THE SYSTEM SHALL mark the card Missed, list it under "Missed the planned day", flag it rolled-over / late (`wo_schedule.is_rolled_over`) and, in the same idempotent daily job (00:30 WIB, NFR-14, BR-13), move the card forward to the next day (`wo_schedule.planned_date` = the next day, logged as Moved in `wo_schedule_log`), without changing the WO. THE SYSTEM SHALL repeat this roll-forward each day, with no limit, until the WO is Completed or Cancelled; the rolled-over / late flag SHALL show on the Planner's schedule card and SHALL be surfaced in the Mechanic mobile task list (REQ-MOB-010). The only consequence of each overrun is the OFF-unit cost (SM-VEH OFF, `work_orders.off_days`, REQ-BIL-003, DEC-92); the schedule still never blocks or cancels the WO (BR-13). | [`MaintenanceSchedule`](<../../../Design Artifact/schedule/Schedule.html#MaintenanceSchedule>) | `wo_schedule.is_missed`, `wo_schedule.is_rolled_over`, `wo_schedule_log` | BR-13, DEC-92 |
| REQ-SCH-004 | P0 | WHEN a card is placed, moved or missed, and each morning for today's list, THE SYSTEM SHALL alert the Field Coordinator in the app (and by push from R1 step 7). | [`NotificationCenter`](<../../../Design Artifact/shell/Notifications.html#NotificationCenter>), [`DispatcherMobileAlerts`](<../../../Design Artifact/mobile/MobileApp.html#DispatcherMobileAlerts>) | `schedule_alerts`, `notifications` |  |
| REQ-SCH-005 | P0 | THE SYSTEM SHALL show the same calendar read only to the Field Coordinator, Supervisor and GM, and the planned day of each unavailable vehicle on the Field Coordinator dashboard. | [`ScheduleDispatcher`](<../../../Design Artifact/schedule/Schedule.html#ScheduleDispatcher>), [`DashboardDispatcher`](<../../../Design Artifact/dashboard/Dashboard.html#DashboardDispatcher>) | `wo_schedule` |  |
| REQ-SCH-006 | P0 | THE SYSTEM SHALL log every placement and move (WO, old day, new day, user, time); a Completed or Cancelled WO leaves the open lists and stays visible on its last day. | [`MaintenanceSchedule`](<../../../Design Artifact/schedule/Schedule.html#MaintenanceSchedule>) | `wo_schedule_log` |  |
| REQ-SCH-007 | P0 | WHEN a WO card is marked Missed, and every morning after that until the WO is Completed, Cancelled or moved, THE SYSTEM SHALL send a late alert to the WO creator, the executor of its open step, the Planners, the Field Coordinators and the GM. | [NotificationCenter](<../../../Design Artifact/shell/Notifications.html#NotificationCenter>) | notifications, wo\_schedule | BR-13 |
