# 4.23 BIL: customers and invoices (P0)

> Part of the BMI Stooring System spec (§4 functional requirements, area BIL). Core rules, roles, state machines, business rules and decisions: [PRD.md](../../../PRD.md). Section numbers and IDs are unchanged from the single-file PRD v2.4.


Goal: every completed SPK is billed to the customer that owns the vehicle, with one invoice per vehicle per day holding every SPK completed for it that day (DEC-43). Accounting and the Supervisor own invoices; the GM views them (`RL-8`). States: `SM-INV` (3.7).
