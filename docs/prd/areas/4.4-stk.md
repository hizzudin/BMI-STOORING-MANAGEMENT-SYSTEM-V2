# 4.4 STK: Stock step (P0, risk: high)

> Part of the BMI Stooring System spec (§4 functional requirements, area STK). Core rules, roles, state machines, business rules and decisions: [PRD.md](../../../PRD.md). Section numbers and IDs are unchanged from the single-file PRD v2.4.


Goal: record the parts, tyres and prices an SPK uses, and cut warehouse stock when they come from the warehouse.

| ID | P | Requirement | Screens | Data | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| REQ-STK-001 | P0 | THE SYSTEM SHALL record in the Stock step only parts and tyres issued from the warehouse (cut stock); anything bought outside, from a shop or a workshop, is recorded in the separate Part Purchase step (4.4a, DEC-60). | [`Stock`](<../../../Design Artifact/steps/Stock.html#Stock>), [`PartPurchase`](<../../../Design Artifact/steps/PartPurchase.html#PartPurchase>) | `spk_parts.source` |  |
| REQ-STK-002 | P0 | THE SYSTEM SHALL let the executor choose only parts and tyre sizes on the SPK's required list; IF the list is wrong, THEN the executor SHALL raise a change request (`REQ-CR-001`) and cannot edit the list. | [`Stock`](<../../../Design Artifact/steps/Stock.html#Stock>), [`ChangeRequest`](<../../../Design Artifact/spk/ChangeRequest.html#ChangeRequest>) | `spk_parts.planned_qty` | BR-02 |
| REQ-STK-003 | P0 | WHEN a Stock step with Warehouse lines is submitted, THE SYSTEM SHALL, in one transaction, write the Goods Issue, reduce stock, write the stock card, and save each line at the moving average cost; IF any part fails, THEN nothing SHALL change. | [`Stock`](<../../../Design Artifact/steps/Stock.html#Stock>) | `goods_issues`, `goods_issue_items`, `parts_catalog`, `stock_cards`, `spk_parts` | BR-06, BR-07, NFR-01 |
| REQ-STK-004 | P0 | IF the quantity to issue is more than the stock on hand, THEN THE SYSTEM SHALL refuse the missing quantity and let the Warehouse Manager confirm a shortfall, which adds a Part Purchase step to the SPK (REQ-PUR-008) without a change request. | [`Stock`](<../../../Design Artifact/steps/Stock.html#Stock>) | `parts_catalog.stock_qty` |  |
| REQ-STK-006 | P0 | THE SYSTEM SHALL require, for each tyre line from the warehouse, the choice of one tyre per unit by code and serial number from tyres In\_Stock, and WHEN the step is submitted, set each chosen tyre Issued to this SPK. | [`Stock`](<../../../Design Artifact/steps/Stock.html#Stock>) | `tyres.issued_spk_id`, `spk_parts.tyre_id`, `tyre_events` | BR-11 |
| REQ-STK-008 | P0 | WHEN the Warehouse Manager submits a reversal of a posted line with a reason, THE SYSTEM SHALL hold it until a Planner or System Admin verifies it, then return the stock at the original unit cost (and Issued tyres to In\_Stock). | [`StockReversal`](<../../../Design Artifact/steps/Stock.html#StockReversal>), [`StepVerify`](<../../../Design Artifact/steps/StepVerify.html#StepVerify>) | `goods_issues.reverses_gi_id`, `spk_parts.reverses_line_id` | BR-06 |
| REQ-STK-009 | P0 | THE SYSTEM SHALL accept invoice images on every Stock step transaction. | [`Stock`](<../../../Design Artifact/steps/Stock.html#Stock>) | `spk_attachments` | NFR-06 |
| REQ-STK-011 | P0 | WHEN a Stock step opens, THE SYSTEM SHALL notify the Warehouse Manager; WHEN a Part Purchase step opens, THE SYSTEM SHALL send a purchase alert to the Field Coordinators and the System Admin with the suggested items. | [`NotificationCenter`](<../../../Design Artifact/shell/Notifications.html#NotificationCenter>) | `notifications` |  |

| ID | Verifies | Acceptance criterion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AC-STK-001 | REQ-STK-004 | IF an SPK needs 8 units and 5 are in stock, THEN submit SHALL be refused, stock SHALL stay 5 and no Goods Issue SHALL exist. |
| AC-STK-003 | REQ-STK-002 | A part not on the SPK list SHALL NOT be selectable. |
| AC-STK-004 | REQ-STK-006 | WHEN a tyre TY-0110 is chosen and the step submitted, TY-0110 SHALL be Issued with `issued_spk_id` = this SPK. |
| AC-STK-005 | REQ-STK-008 | WHEN a reversal is submitted, stock SHALL stay unchanged until the reversal is verified. |
| AC-STK-006 | REQ-STK-004, REQ-PUR-008 | WHEN an SPK needs 8 L of engine oil and 5 L are in stock, the Warehouse Manager issues 5 L and confirms a shortfall of 3 L, THE SYSTEM SHALL write one Goods Issue for 5 L, add a Part Purchase step after the Stock step with "Engine oil 15W-40, 3 L" suggested, and alert the Field Coordinators. |
