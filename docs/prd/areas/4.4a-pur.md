# 4.4a PUR: Part Purchase step (P0)

> Part of the BMI Stooring System spec (§4 functional requirements, area PUR). Core rules, roles, state machines, business rules and decisions: [PRD.md](../../../PRD.md). Section numbers and IDs are unchanged from the single-file PRD v2.4.


Goal: record parts bought outside for one SPK as they appear on the shop's receipt, so the buyer can check the receipt line by line (DEC-60). The step never touches warehouse stock.

| ID | P | Requirement | Screens | Data | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| REQ-PUR-001 | P0 | THE SYSTEM SHALL register Part Purchase as its own module, executed by a Field Coordinator, Warehouse Manager or System Admin; an SPK MAY hold it after a Stock step or without one. | [`CreateSPK`](<../../../Design Artifact/spk/CreateSPK.html#CreateSPK>), [`PartPurchase`](<../../../Design Artifact/steps/PartPurchase.html#PartPurchase>), [`FCMobilePurchase`](<../../../Design Artifact/mobile/PartPurchase.html#FCMobilePurchase>) | `assignment_modules`, `spk_purchases` | REQ-REG-002 |
| REQ-PUR-002 | P0 | THE SYSTEM SHALL fill the step's lines with the parts to buy that the Planner or System Admin listed on the SPK (part only, no quantity) and the shortfall parts of the Stock step (the missing quantity shown as a hint). The executor SHALL type the quantity actually bought, which MAY be more than the hint, and the unit price of each line, and SHALL NOT add, remove or change parts; a wrong or missing part needs a change request (DEC-63, DEC-64). | [`PartPurchase`](<../../../Design Artifact/steps/PartPurchase.html#PartPurchase>), [`FCMobilePurchase`](<../../../Design Artifact/mobile/PartPurchase.html#FCMobilePurchase>) | `spk_purchase_lines` | BR-02 |
| REQ-PUR-003 | P0 | THE SYSTEM SHALL show each line total (quantity x unit price) and a large calculated total that update as the executor types, so the buyer can check each line against the receipt; there is no receipt total field and no match check (DEC-61). The step verifier SHALL compare the lines and the total with the receipt photo and, IF they do not match, THEN return the step to the executor with a note (REQ-ENG-005). | [`PartPurchase`](<../../../Design Artifact/steps/PartPurchase.html#PartPurchase>), [`FCMobilePurchase`](<../../../Design Artifact/mobile/PartPurchase.html#FCMobilePurchase>) | `spk_purchases.calculated_total` | NFR-16 |
| REQ-PUR-004 | P0 | THE SYSTEM SHALL require a vendor (from the vendor master, or typed by hand as Other), the receipt date and at least one receipt photo; the receipt number is optional. | [`PartPurchase`](<../../../Design Artifact/steps/PartPurchase.html#PartPurchase>) | `spk_purchases`, `spk_attachments` | NFR-06 |
| REQ-PUR-005 | P0 | WHERE a line is a Tyre, THE SYSTEM SHALL also require serial number, brand, size, type and tread, and on submit create a new tyre with a generated code and status Issued to this SPK, so it can be mounted in Part Replacement. | [`PartPurchase`](<../../../Design Artifact/steps/PartPurchase.html#PartPurchase>) | `tyres`, `spk_purchase_lines.tyre_id` | BR-11 |
| REQ-PUR-006 | P0 | WHEN the step is submitted, THE SYSTEM SHALL add the calculated total to the SPK parts cost and never change warehouse stock or the stock card; the lines are billed with the part markup (REQ-BIL-003). | [`PartPurchase`](<../../../Design Artifact/steps/PartPurchase.html#PartPurchase>) | `spks.parts_cost` | BR-06, TX-21 |
| REQ-PUR-007 | P0 | IF nothing had to be bought, THEN THE SYSTEM SHALL let the executor submit the step with no lines and a reason. | [`PartPurchase`](<../../../Design Artifact/steps/PartPurchase.html#PartPurchase>) | `spk_purchases.nothing_bought_reason` | - |
| REQ-PUR-008 | P0 | WHEN the Warehouse Manager confirms a shortfall in a Stock step and the SPK has no open Part Purchase step after it, THE SYSTEM SHALL add one right after the Stock step, with the shortfall quantities as its lines, and send the purchase alert. | [`Stock`](<../../../Design Artifact/steps/Stock.html#Stock>), [`PartPurchase`](<../../../Design Artifact/steps/PartPurchase.html#PartPurchase>) | `spk_assignments` | REQ-STK-004 |
| REQ-PUR-009 | P0 | THE SYSTEM SHALL treat each purchased line like an issued part of the same SKU: WHEN the SPK completes, it resets the linked part items (REQ-BSL-006) and computes life used and the early failure flag (REQ-BSL-009) from the catalog's normal life (DEC-62). | - | - | REQ-BSL-006 |
