# 4.5 MEC: Mechanic Action step (P0)

> Part of the BMI Stooring System spec (§4 functional requirements, area MEC). Core rules, roles, state machines, business rules and decisions: [PRD.md](../../../PRD.md). Section numbers and IDs are unchanged from the single-file PRD v2.4.


Goal: record the services performed, with proof and price.

| ID | P | Requirement | Screens | Data | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| REQ-MEC-001 | P0 | THE SYSTEM SHALL require the executor to choose the team, Internal or External, before filling the step. | [`Mechanic`](<../../../Design Artifact/steps/Mechanic.html#Mechanic>) | `spk_assignments.team` |  |
| REQ-MEC-002 | P0 | WHILE the team is Internal, THE SYSTEM SHALL take service and price only from the service catalog (price read only) and require at least one repair photo. | [`Mechanic`](<../../../Design Artifact/steps/Mechanic.html#Mechanic>) | `spk_services`, `service_catalog`, `spk_attachments` | BR-07 |
| REQ-MEC-003 | P0 | WHILE the team is External, THE SYSTEM SHALL take vendor, description and a free price, and require at least one invoice image. | [`MechanicExternal`](<../../../Design Artifact/steps/MechanicExternal.html#MechanicExternal>) | `spk_services` |  |
| REQ-MEC-004 | P0 | THE SYSTEM SHALL compute labor cost as the sum of service lines and SPK total cost as labor + parts + document renewal cost + stooring cost (REQ-MBL-006). | [`SPKDetail`](<../../../Design Artifact/spk/SPKDetail.html#SPKDetail>) | `spks.labor_cost`, `parts_cost`, `document_cost` |  |
| REQ-MEC-005 | P0 | THE SYSTEM SHALL let a System Admin maintain the service catalog (code, name, standard price, active, optional part item type). | [`SettingsServiceCatalog`](<../../../Design Artifact/settings/SettingsServiceCatalog.html#SettingsServiceCatalog>) | `service_catalog` |  |

| REQ-CHK-001 | P0 | THE SYSTEM SHALL offer a Part Check module (troubleshooting) in Create SPK: the Planner or System Admin lists at least one part to check (catalog part, part item type, tyre position or a described part) with what to check. | REQ-CHK-002 | P0 | For every listed part the executor SHALL write its condition, decide Good, Repair or Replace, and add at least one photo; a part marked Repair or Replace MAY be marked Unsafe to drive. The executor MAY add parts that were not listed, shown as Added by mechanic, under the same rules. Submit SHALL be refused until every part is complete. | [`PartCheck`](<../../../Design Artifact/steps/PartCheck.html#PartCheck>), [`MechanicMobileCheck`](<../../../Design Artifact/mobile/MechanicMobileCheck.html#MechanicMobileCheck>) | `spk_check_items`, `spk_attachments` | REQ-FUP rules for photos |
| REQ-CHK-003 | P0 | WHEN a Part Check step is verified and at least one part is Repair or Replace, THE SYSTEM SHALL create one Draft SPK (source Part\_Check, linked to the step) for the same vehicle with the not-good findings and links to their photos: category Corrective (Tyre when only tyres), the workshop flag of the check SPK, priority Urgent when a part is Unsafe to drive, otherwise the check SPK's priority; steps built from the findings: a Replace part is a Stock line when in stock or a Part Purchase line when not, a tyre to replace adds a Part Replacement step for its position, and each Repair is a line of work in a Mechanic Action step. No draft is made when every part is Good; a returned step makes no draft. | [`PartCheckVerify`](<../../../Design Artifact/steps/PartCheckVerify.html#PartCheckVerify>), [`CreateSPKFromCheck`](<../../../Design Artifact/spk/CreateSPKFromCheck.html#CreateSPKFromCheck>) | `spks`, `spk_assignments` | REQ-SPK-001: never Active |
| REQ-CHK-004 | P0 | THE SYSTEM SHALL show the follow-up draft to the Planner and System Admin with the findings carried over; they MAY change its steps as in any SPK, then Activate it (submit for approval, so it becomes Active only after a Supervisor or GM approves) or Discard it with a reason. A discarded draft SHALL keep its link to the check and SHALL NOT be made again for the same step. | [`CreateSPKFromCheck`](<../../../Design Artifact/spk/CreateSPKFromCheck.html#CreateSPKFromCheck>), [`DashboardPlanner`](<../../../Design Artifact/dashboard/DashboardPlanner.html#DashboardPlanner>) | `spks` | BR-03 applies |
| REQ-CHK-005 | P0 | WHEN a Part Check step with a part marked Unsafe to drive is submitted, THE SYSTEM SHALL notify the Planner, Supervisor and Field Coordinators and show an Unsafe to drive warning on the vehicle and in the schedule until the follow-up SPK is completed or discarded; it SHALL NOT block the vehicle on its own. | [`Vehicles`](<../../../Design Artifact/fleet/Vehicles.html#Vehicles>), [`NotificationCenter`](<../../../Design Artifact/shell/NotificationCenter.html#NotificationCenter>) | `spk_check_items` | DEC-88 |
| REQ-CHK-006 | P0 | THE SPK document SHALL print a Part Check section with each part, what was checked, the condition, the decision, the Unsafe to drive mark and the photo file names (photos are not printed), the check service (the stooring of a field check is printed with the SPK's Mobilisasi step, REQ-MBL-006), the stamps and the execution time, and the number and status of the follow-up SPK for reference only. The check SPK SHALL stand alone: it is approved, completed, documented and invoiced on its own whatever happens to the follow-up SPK, and its document and invoice SHALL show no line of the follow-up SPK. The follow-up SPK's document SHALL name the check it came from. | [`SPKDocument`](<../../../Design Artifact/spk/SPKDocument.html#SPKDocument>) | `spk_check_items` | REQ-SPKD-002 |
| REQ-CHK-007 | P0 | THE Part Check step SHALL carry its check service as labor, billed to the customer with the check SPK: for an internal team, at least one service from the catalog at its fixed price (category Diagnosis, for example SVC-095 Brake system check); for an external workshop, vendor, description, price and the invoice image. Its stooring area and fee are produced by the Mobilisasi module of the same SPK when the check is done in the field (`REQ-MBL-006`), not by the Part Check step. Repairs found by the check are billed with the follow-up SPK, never with the check. | [`PartCheck`](<../../../Design Artifact/steps/PartCheck.html#PartCheck>), [`SettingsServiceCatalog`](<../../../Design Artifact/settings/SettingsServiceCatalog.html#SettingsServiceCatalog>), [`InvoiceDetail`](<../../../Design Artifact/billing/InvoiceDetail.html#InvoiceDetail>) | `spk_services`, `service_catalog` | DEC-89, BR-07 |
