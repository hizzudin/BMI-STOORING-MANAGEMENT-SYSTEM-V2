# 4.9 SPKD: SPK digital document (P0)

> Part of the BMI Stooring System spec (§4 functional requirements, area SPKD). Core rules, roles, state machines, business rules and decisions: [PRD.md](../../../PRD.md). Section numbers and IDs are unchanged from the single-file PRD v2.4.


| ID | P | Requirement | Screens | Data | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| REQ-SPKD-001 | P0 | THE SYSTEM SHALL produce for every SPK a digital document, printable and exportable to PDF. | [`SPKDocument`](<../../../Design Artifact/spk/SPKDocument.html#SPKDocument>) |  |  |
| REQ-SPKD-002 | P0 | THE SYSTEM SHALL include the SPK header (number, type, source, priority, status), the description, the vehicle (plate, VIN, brand, model, year, odometer at creation, tyre count, axle count, customer), one completion section per step as defined by its module, an executor stamp and a verifier stamp per step, the placement changes in the Mechanic Action section (as REQ-SPKD-007 prints them), every step timestamp, the total active time and the cost, with the stooring area and fee of each Mobilisasi step (never the mechanic's location trail). | [`SPKDocument`](<../../../Design Artifact/spk/SPKDocument.html#SPKDocument>) | `assignment_modules.completion_section` |  |
| REQ-SPKD-003 | P0 | THE SYSTEM SHALL end the document with an approval block "Approved by GM/Supervisor" with two lines, creation and completion, each with stamp or signature, name, role (Supervisor, GM or System Admin), date and time, and the mark Self-approved where it applies; the completion line stays empty until granted. | [`SPKDocument`](<../../../Design Artifact/spk/SPKDocument.html#SPKDocument>), [`SPKDetail`](<../../../Design Artifact/spk/SPKDetail.html#SPKDetail>) | `spk_approvals` |  |
| REQ-SPKD-004 | P0 | IF anyone tries to change a stamp after it is applied, THEN THE SYSTEM SHALL refuse. |  | `spk_approvals` (append-only) | BR-10 |

| REQ-SPKD-006 | P1 | THE SYSTEM SHALL attach the amendment history to the document. | [`SPKDocument`](<../../../Design Artifact/spk/SPKDocument.html#SPKDocument>) | `spk_change_requests` |  |
| REQ-SPKD-007 | P0 | THE SYSTEM SHALL keep the SPK document print-ready in black and white: it SHALL contain no photos and no colour images; each step SHALL list its attachments as text (file name, type, uploaded by, time); statuses SHALL print as words; the placement snapshot SHALL print as a black-and-white line drawing with a table of changed positions (DEC-33). | [SPKDocument](<../../../Design Artifact/spk/SPKDocument.html#SPKDocument>) | spk\_attachments | DEC-33 |
| REQ-SPKD-008 | P0 | THE SPK document SHALL show for each step the opened, started, submitted and verified times and its pauses with reasons, and an Execution time table: per step the time waited to start, worked, paused, waited for verification and the number of returns, plus the approval waits of creation and completion; the longest phase and entered start times SHALL be marked. These times SHALL NOT appear on any invoice. | [`SPKDocument`](<../../../Design Artifact/spk/SPKDocument.html#SPKDocument>) | `step_time_events` | DEC-87 |
